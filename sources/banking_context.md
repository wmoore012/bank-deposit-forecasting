# Banking context and limits

Reviewed September 21, 2026. These sources support the banking explanation; model performance comes from the saved experiment outputs.

- [Bank of England: Money creation in the modern economy](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/quarterly-bulletin/2014/q1/money-creation-in-the-modern-economy). Lending can create deposits. The opening balance sheet is a deliberately simplified numerical illustration of cash availability and equity.
- [Federal Reserve: Assessing Bank Resilience to a Funding Shock](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/assessing-bank-resilience-to-a-funding-shock-20260217.html). A modeled funding shock supplies context for replacement funding costs; it does not validate this project's screening performance.
- [New York DFS bulletin, November 22, 2024](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/reports-and-publications/weekly-bulletins/wb20241122). Emigrant Bank merged into Plus International under the Emigrant name effective October 1, 2023. The source was published after the event. Approval dates in the FDIC merger decisions table are different milestones.
- [Silvergate March 8, 2023 announcement filed with SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1312109/000131210923000058/ex991sipressrelease3x8x23.htm). Announces intended wind-down and voluntary liquidation.
- [Silvergate November 22, 2023 announcement filed with SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1312109/000095015723001160/ex99-1.htm). Reports repayment of deposit liabilities except amounts below $10,000. This bounds the documented sensitivity window; subsequent activity is unclassified.
- [FFIEC UBPR FAQ](https://cdr.ffiec.gov/public/HelpFiles/FAQ.htm). Explains comparisons with a bank's own history and peer groups. Detailed contemporary peer definitions are deliberately omitted; any future historical peer analysis requires definitions valid during that period.
- [Federal Reserve SR 26-2](https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/SR2602.htm). Issued April 17, 2026, supersedes SR 11-7 and SR 21-8. The Fed says it is most relevant to organizations above $30 billion. Used here as conceptual background, without a compliance claim.

The 10% review capacity is an experiment assumption. The forecast orders reported deposit growth. Bank health, causes, supervisory actions, and realized savings require additional evidence.
